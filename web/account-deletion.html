Delete Your DYOS Account

Effective Date: August 9, 2026

You can permanently delete your DYOS account and all associated data directly from within the app. No web form or waiting period is required.

How to delete your account in the app

  1. Open the DYOS app and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Review the warning and confirm by tapping Delete Forever.

What gets deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes:

  • Your profile, email, and authentication credentials.
  • Your access to your shared couple data. If you are paired with a partner, your partner's account and their own data are not deleted — only your access and personally-identifying data are removed.
  • User-generated content that is exclusively yours (e.g. your own notes and account-level settings).
  • Device tokens used for push notifications.

Shared content such as joint timeline memories may be retained on your (former) partner's account, consistent with the fact that it was created jointly. See our Privacy Policy for full details on data retention.

Can't access the app?

If you no longer have access to the app (e.g. lost device, can't sign in), you can request deletion by emailing us with the email address associated with your account:
