Child Safety Standards

Last updated: April 9, 2026

DYOS is a private relationship application designed exclusively for adult couples. We take our responsibility to protect minors with the utmost seriousness. This document outlines our standards and policies regarding child safety, including our zero-tolerance stance against child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE).

1. Platform Age Requirement

DYOS is intended solely for users who are 18 years of age or older. By creating an account, users confirm they meet this minimum age requirement.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If we discover that a user under the age of 18 has registered, we will immediately suspend and delete the account and all associated data.

2. Zero Tolerance for CSAE

DYOS maintains a strict zero-tolerance policy against child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) in any form. This includes, but is not limited to:

  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) in any format
  • Content that sexualizes minors, including illustrated or AI-generated material
  • Grooming behaviors or communications targeting minors
  • Solicitation of minors for sexual purposes
  • Sharing, distributing, or linking to any CSAM

Any account found violating these standards will be immediately and permanently banned. We reserve the right to report violations to relevant law enforcement authorities and the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC).

3. Content Moderation & Detection

While DYOS is a private, end-to-end encrypted application, we employ the following safeguards to detect and prevent CSAE:

  • Age verification prompts during account registration
  • User reporting mechanisms available in-app for flagging abuse
  • Prompt investigation and response to all safety reports
  • Cooperation with law enforcement upon receipt of valid legal process

4. Reporting Child Safety Concerns

If you encounter any content or behavior that you believe involves child sexual abuse or exploitation, please report it immediately using one of the following channels:

DYOS Safety Team

Email: safety@dyos.app

We aim to respond to all safety reports within 24 hours.

External Reporting Resources

  • NCMEC CyberTipline (USA)
  • Internet Watch Foundation (EU/UK)
  • Europol – Report Cybercrime

5. Law Enforcement Cooperation

DYOS will fully cooperate with law enforcement agencies and child protection organizations investigating child sexual abuse and exploitation. Upon receipt of valid legal process (warrant, court order, or equivalent), we will provide all available information relevant to the investigation, to the extent permitted by law and technically feasible given our encryption architecture.

6. Policy Updates

This Child Safety Standards document will be reviewed and updated periodically to reflect changes in applicable laws, platform features, and industry best practices. Significant changes will be communicated to users via the application and/or email. Continued use of DYOS constitutes acceptance of the current version of this policy.

Have a Safety Concern?

Our safety team is available 24/7 for urgent child safety reports.
