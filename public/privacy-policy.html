Privacy Policy for DYOS

Effective Date: March 23, 2026

Welcome to DYOS. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your most personal memories, notes, and relationship data remain secure. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and related services (collectively, the "App").

1. Information We Collect

Because DYOS is designed to be a private space for you and your partner, we collect information necessary to connect you and provide our features.

A. Information You Provide to Us

Account Data: When you register via email or Google Sign-In, we collect your email address, password, name, and profile picture.

Pairing Data: We generate and process inviteCodes and coupleIds to link your account securely with your partner's account.

User-Generated Content: This includes text, photos, and media you upload to your shared timeline (Memories), as well as notes, lists, and calendar events.

Sensitive Health and Personal Data: If you choose to use our tracking features, we collect data regarding your intimacy logs and menstrual cycle (Cycle and Intimacy Trackers). This data is highly sensitive and is only shared with your paired partner.

Communication Data: Quick messages and haptic signals sent between you and your partner.

B. Information Collected Automatically

Device and Usage Data: We collect push notification tokens (FCM tokens) to send you alerts, as well as gamification data (XP, levels, and unlocks) based on your app usage.

Location Data: If you add locations to your memories, we process geographic information via the Google Places API to display your "Memories Map."

Payment Information: If you upgrade to our Premium tier, your purchases are securely processed through third-party app stores (Apple App Store / Google Play) and managed via RevenueCat. We do not collect or store your actual credit card details, only your subscription status (subscriptionTier, subscriptionExpiry).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data strictly to provide and improve the DYOS experience:

  • To provide the service: to authenticate your account, pair you with your partner, and synchronize your shared timeline, trackers, and notes in real-time.
  • To communicate: to send you push notifications (e.g., when your partner adds a memory or sends a haptic signal) and administrative emails.
  • To manage subscriptions: to unlock Premium features for your couple based on your purchase history.
  • To improve the app: to troubleshoot technical issues, monitor app performance, and prevent fraud or abuse (e.g., via rate limiting and App Check).

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal data. Your data is shared only in the following ways:

  • With Your Partner: By entering an invite code, you explicitly consent to sharing your profile, timeline, trackers, notes, and events with your paired partner.
  • With Service Providers: We use trusted third-party infrastructure to run DYOS securely. These include Google/Firebase, RevenueCat, and Google Places API.
  • For Legal Reasons: We may disclose your information if required to do so by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities.

4. Data Security

We take the security of your intimate data very seriously. We employ industry-standard measures to protect your information, including:

  • Access Control: Strict backend security rules (Firestore and Storage rules) ensure that your data can only be read and modified by you and your paired partner.
  • Encryption: All data is encrypted in transit and at rest using our cloud providers' native encryption standards.
  • Transaction Security: Crucial actions, like pairing accounts, are handled via secure, server-side transactions to prevent unauthorized access.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

We keep your personal information only for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you the App's services.

Your Right to Delete: You can request the deletion of your account and all associated data at any time through the App's settings or by contacting us. If you or your partner delete a shared memory or note, it is permanently removed from our active servers. If a couple unpairs, shared access is immediately revoked.

6. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Delete your personal data.
  • Withdraw Consent for processing sensitive data (e.g., cycle or intimacy tracking) at any time by stopping the use of those features or deleting the data within the app.

7. Children's Privacy

DYOS is designed for adults and couples. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under the age of 18. If we become aware that we have collected data from a minor, we will take steps to delete that information immediately.

8. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this policy and, if the changes are significant, by providing a prominent notice within the App.
